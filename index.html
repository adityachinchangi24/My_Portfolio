03 / RESEARCH

Independent research direction

Sparse Geometric Feature Representation

Lightweight Edge-Based Spatial Perception

What if a perception system could keep the structure that matters without continuously retaining the complete image?

This research direction explores a representation layer between a camera and an AI system. Instead of treating the raw image as the primary representation, the proposed pipeline extracts useful structure such as key points, coordinates, geometric descriptors and spatial relationships.

Camera → Feature Extraction → Key Points → X/Y/Z Geometry → Sparse Representation → Local AI

Research questions

  • What information can be retained without keeping every pixel?
  • How should objects and spatial relationships be represented?
  • How much can representation size be reduced?
  • What happens to recognition accuracy and latency?

Potential benefits to evaluate

  • Lower storage and bandwidth requirements
  • Local or offline processing
  • Reduced computational load
  • Potential privacy advantages

These are research hypotheses and design goals, not proven performance claims. Experiments are required to establish compression, accuracy, latency, memory, energy and privacy characteristics.

Application concept: Assistive navigation for visually impaired users is one potential application of the underlying representation, not a separate project.
